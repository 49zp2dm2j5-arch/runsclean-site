03 / Who's behind it

A publisher, not a persona.

Runs Clean publishes under its own name. There's no invented instructor with a stock headshot and fifteen years of made-up experience. What we put forward instead is the standard the courses are held to, and you can check it yourself, because every lab ships with its tests.

The narration uses a synthetic voice, and we say so on every course page. The technical content doesn't get that shortcut: claims are sourced and dated, labs pass before anything is recorded, and the course tells you which spec revision it covers.
